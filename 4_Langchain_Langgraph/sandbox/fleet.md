# Electric Vehicle Comparison for Sales Fleet

## Candidates:
- **Tata Motors Nexon.ev**
- **Mahindra XEV 9S**
- **Maruti Suzuki e Vitara**

### Tata Motors Nexon.ev
- **Price**: ₹12.49 lakh (base model)
- **Battery Options**: 30 kWh (range: 325 km) / 45 kWh (range: 489 km)
- **Power**: 127 - 142 bhp
- **Torque**: 215 Nm
- **Charging Time**: 10 to 80% in about 56 minutes using 50 kW charger.
- **Safety Features**: 5-star safety rating, multiple airbags.
- **Warranty**: 8 years or 160,000 km on battery.

### Mahindra XEV 9S
- **Price**: ₹20.65 lakh - ₹30.90 lakh 
- **Battery Options**: 70 kWh (241 bhp), 79 kWh (228 bhp)
- **Range**: 521 km - 679 km depending on battery pack
- **Safety Features**: Level 2 ADAS, 16-speaker audio system.
- **Seating**: 7 seats, ideal for larger groups or families.
- **Display Features**: Three 12.3-inch screens.

### Maruti Suzuki e Vitara
- **Price**: ₹15.99 lakh - ₹20.01 lakh 
- **Battery Options**: 49 kWh (range: 440 km) / 61 kWh (range: 543 km)
- **Power**: 172 bhp
- **Torque**: 193 Nm
- **Charging Time**: 10-100% in about 9 hours with AC charger.
- **Safety Features**: Standard 7 airbags across variants.
- **Boot Space**: 310-liters providing practical cargo capacity.

## Recommendation
After reviewing the specifications and features of each vehicle, the **Tata Nexon.ev** stands out as the most cost-effective option for fleet use with a strong blend of efficiency, performance, and safety at a competitive price point. The **Mahindra XEV 9S** offers higher range and capacity but may be priced out of budget for larger quantities. The **Maruti Suzuki e Vitara** also presents a solid option, providing a good balance of features and cost. However, for the majority of budget constraints and operational efficiency, **Tata Nexon.ev** is the recommended choice.
