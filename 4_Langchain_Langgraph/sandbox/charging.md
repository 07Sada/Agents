# Electric Vehicle Charging Landscape in India

## Overview of Public Charging Points
As of June 2023, India has approximately **8,738 operational public EV charging stations**. This reflects a significant expansion of the charging infrastructure, with a growing number of stations catering to the increasing demand for electric vehicles (EVs). However, despite this growth, the current ratio indicates that there is only **one public charging station for every 235 EVs**, which can lead to long wait times for fleet operators.

## Major Charging Networks
### 1. Statiq
Statiq is recognized as **India's largest EV charging network**, boasting over **7,000 charging stations** installed across **63+ cities**. This widespread coverage makes it a suitable choice for fleets looking for reliable charging options, particularly in urban centers. Statiq's chargers include both fast and slow charging options, ensuring that users can quickly recharge vehicles as needed.

### 2. Tata Power
Tata Power has emerged as another major player in the EV charging landscape, with a network that includes around **2,000 EV charging stations** throughout the country. The company is known for its extensive network of fast charging stations and has partnered with various stakeholders to enhance its infrastructure. Tata Power's commitment to sustainability and innovative solutions positions it well for supporting fleet operations effectively.
