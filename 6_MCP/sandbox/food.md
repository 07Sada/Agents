# Palak Paneer

Source: [Swasthi's Recipes – Palak Paneer Recipe](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/palak-paneer-recipe-easy-paneer-recipes-step-by-step-pics/)

## Why this recipe is great
- Uses fresh spinach and avoids overcooking for better color and flavor.
- Skips blanching by sautéing spinach first, which helps keep the taste bright.
- Balanced with whole spices, kasuri methi, and a small amount of cream (optional).

## Key ingredients
- Paneer
- Fresh spinach (palak)
- Onions
- Tomatoes
- Green chilies
- Ginger-garlic paste
- Butter + oil
- Whole spices: cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, cumin
- Garam masala
- Kasuri methi
- Cream (optional)

## Quick summary
1. Clean and sauté spinach with green chilies until wilted.
2. Blend to a smooth, thick puree.
3. Make a base with butter, oil, whole spices, onions, ginger-garlic paste, and tomatoes.
4. Add garam masala, water, and kasuri methi; cook until the gravy is thick.
5. Stir in spinach puree briefly, then add paneer cubes.
6. Finish with cream if desired and serve hot.

## Tips
- Use young, tender spinach to avoid bitterness.
- Don’t overcook the spinach puree.
- Soak store-bought paneer in hot water for a softer texture.

## Serve with
- Naan
- Roti
- Basmati rice
